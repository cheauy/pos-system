# Fashion storefront banner

Generation prompt:

> Generate a photorealistic fashion boutique website hero banner, wide landscape 3:1 aspect ratio. Dark midnight navy modern boutique interior, beautiful neat row of cream, heather grey, white and black knit sweaters and t-shirts on natural oak wooden hangers suspended on a black metal clothing rail on the RIGHT HALF. A softly blurred leafy plant near center, elegant soft window lighting, rich editorial photographic texture. LEFT HALF almost empty dark navy wall with subtle tonal depth, suitable for white headline overlay in HTML. No logos, no words, no lettering, no captions, no user interface. Quiet premium casual clothing campaign. The rack fills right half and extends off lower edge. Main garments centrally vertically positioned so center-cropping is safe.
